1/*
2 * @author Tedd OKANO
3 *
4 * Released under the MIT license
5 *
6 * TimerOne's interface: a timer with a period in microseconds that can
7 * call a function from its interrupt. Written from scratch to the TimerOne
8 * library's interface (none of its source, CC BY 3.0, is copied), so
9 * sketches written for it build unchanged.
10 *
11 * On AVR it runs on the 16-bit Timer1, and its longest period is about
12 * 8.4 s. Here it runs on CTIMER1 on the MCX A and N chips (12 MHz,
13 * longest period about 357 s), and on PIT channel 1 on FRDM-MCXC444 (the
14 * 24 MHz bus clock, about 178 s). A longer period is cut to the longest.
15 * Both count a clock made from the same oscillator as the core clock, so
16 * the periods agree with millis() and micros(), as on AVR.
17 *
18 * The PWM functions (pwm(), setPwmDuty() and disablePwm()) are not
19 * provided: on AVR they drive pins 9 and 10 from Timer1, which have no
20 * counterpart here. Use analogWrite() and analogWriteFrequency() on the
21 * PWM pins instead.
22 */
